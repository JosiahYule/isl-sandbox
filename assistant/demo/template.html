Behind the scenes

How it works

  1. The visitor types what they want, in their own words.
  2. The AI decides what to search for. It turns "I drove a forklift at Sobeys" into keywords like forklift, warehouse, material handler.
  3. Ordinary code runs the search over the live Avionte postings and hands back real matches. The AI can't see or invent jobs outside what comes back.
  4. The AI picks the best fits and explains why, and the chat shows them as cards with View and Apply buttons.
